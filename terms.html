SUBSCRIPTION TERMS

Samson's Automation Subscription & Billing Terms

Last updated: September 20, 2026.

Your checkout controls the exact agreement. Before a business starts or changes a subscription, the administrator is shown the exact monthly price, included package, included features, any amount due immediately, renewal behavior, and a payment-authorization checkbox. The accepted version is stored with the business account.

1. Monthly subscription

Subscriptions renew monthly until canceled. The monthly price displayed at checkout is the amount authorized for future recurring billing for the selected package unless a later package change is separately accepted.

2. Package upgrades

When a business upgrades during an already-paid monthly period, the amount already paid for the current package is credited toward the full monthly price of the upgraded package. The remaining difference is due when the upgrade is activated. Future renewals use the new monthly price.

3. Package downgrades

Lower-priced package changes are scheduled for the next renewal period unless Samson's Automation agrees otherwise. Current paid access remains available through the existing paid period.

4. Cancellation

A cancellation received at least 24 hours before the next scheduled renewal charge will not be charged again. The software disables automatic renewal when cancellation is recorded. Unless otherwise stated, the business retains access through the end of the already-paid period.

5. Payment authorization

By checking the authorization box during checkout, the business administrator confirms authority to accept the stated package, price, included features, and recurring-payment terms on behalf of the business.

6. Payment methods

Automatic recurring Venmo billing, when enabled, uses a payment method securely vaulted by PayPal. Samson's Automation does not store raw Venmo credentials. Cash App and manual Venmo handles may be offered for manual payments; manual payments require confirmation before paid package access is activated.

7. Feature access

Access to Lead Management, Timekeeping, Mobile Clocking, and Payroll Ready may be enabled or disabled based on the purchased package or by a Samson's Automation platform administrator. Package access is enforced server-side.

8. Payroll scope

Payroll Ready is a timekeeping and payroll-handoff workflow. Samson's Automation does not calculate or file payroll taxes, transmit direct deposits, or act as the employer's payroll processor unless a separate written agreement explicitly says otherwise.

9. Contact

Questions about billing or cancellation can be sent to SamsonsAutomations@gmail.com.
